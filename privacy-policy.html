Introduction

About This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Fresh Coat Painters of Cypress (operated by D&JM Multiservices LLC) collects, uses, stores, and protects personal information obtained through our website, lead forms, Facebook lead ads, and SMS communications program. By submitting your information through any of our forms, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

For SMS-specific terms, see our Terms of Service.

Information Collected

What Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of personal information:

  • Contact information — First name, last name, phone number, and email address submitted through estimate request forms or lead ads
  • SMS opt-in consent data — Consent status, opt-in timestamp, opt-in method (web form, Facebook lead ad), and opt-out requests
  • Service inquiry details — Project type, property address, service preferences, and any other details you provide when requesting an estimate
  • Communication history — Records of SMS messages sent and received, email correspondence, and call logs
  • Website usage data — Pages visited, time on site, and interactions collected via cookies and analytics tools
How Data Is Used

How We Use Your Information

We use the personal information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your estimate requests and service inquiries
  • To confirm appointments and deliver project-related updates via SMS and email
  • To send SMS communications you have explicitly consented to receive
  • To send promotional offers and reactivation messages to opted-in contacts
  • To improve our services and customer communications
  • To comply with applicable laws and regulations
  • To maintain records of consent for regulatory compliance

We do not use your information for any purpose beyond what is listed above without your explicit consent.

SMS Opt-In

SMS Opt-In Data & Consent

SMS opt-in consent is collected separately from other personal information. Specifically:

  • Consent is obtained through an explicit checkbox on our estimate request form — phone number submission alone does not constitute SMS consent
  • Transactional SMS consent and marketing SMS consent are collected via separate, distinct checkboxes
  • Neither consent checkbox is pre-checked — consent is always an active, affirmative choice
  • Marketing SMS consent is always optional and never required to submit a form or receive service
  • The opt-in form clearly displays our business name (Fresh Coat Painters of Cypress / D&JM Multiservices LLC), message types, frequency disclosure, data rates disclosure, and STOP/HELP instructions at the point of consent
  • Opt-in consent records including timestamp, method, and form version are stored securely
  • Opt-out requests (STOP) are processed immediately and permanently

No mobile phone numbers or SMS opt-in consent data will ever be shared with, sold to, rented to, or transferred to any third party or affiliate for marketing or promotional purposes. This policy applies without exception.

Cookies & Tracking

Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website and landing pages use cookies and similar tracking technologies to improve user experience and measure marketing effectiveness. These include:

  • Essential cookies — Required for pages to function properly; cannot be disabled without affecting functionality
  • Analytics cookies — Used to understand how visitors interact with our pages (e.g., Google Analytics 4). Data collected is aggregated and anonymized.
  • Advertising pixels — Used to measure the performance of advertising campaigns and build retargeting audiences (e.g., Meta/Facebook Pixel). These cookies track page visits and form submissions for ad performance measurement.
  • Session cookies — Temporary cookies that expire when you close your browser; used to maintain form state and navigation

You may disable cookies through your browser settings at any time. Disabling cookies may affect the functionality of certain pages. We do not use cookies to collect or store SMS opt-in consent data — that is collected only through explicit form submissions.

By using our website or submitting a form, you acknowledge our use of cookies and tracking technologies as described above. If you wish to opt out of analytics tracking, you may use browser extensions such as the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Data Security

Data Security Practices

We implement the following security practices to protect your personal information:

  • Access controls — Customer contact data is accessible only to authorized personnel directly involved in service delivery and communications. Access is role-based and reviewed regularly.
  • Encryption — Personal data is stored within industry-standard CRM and communications platforms that employ encryption both at rest and in transit (TLS/SSL)
  • Third-party processors — Any third-party platforms that process your data (SMS delivery providers, CRM systems) are bound by strict data processing agreements prohibiting unauthorized use of your data
  • Data minimization — We collect only the information necessary to fulfill the purpose for which it was requested
  • Retention limits — We retain personal information only as long as necessary to fulfill the stated purpose or as required by law. Contact data for inactive customers is reviewed and purged periodically.
  • Breach response — In the event of a data breach affecting your information, we will notify affected individuals as required by applicable law within the required timeframe

While we implement reasonable and industry-standard safeguards, no data transmission over the internet or electronic storage system can be guaranteed 100% secure. If you have concerns about the security of your data, contact us immediately at jmartinta@freshcoatpainters.com.

Your Rights

Your Rights & Choices

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Opt out of SMS — Reply STOP to any message at any time to permanently unsubscribe from SMS communications
  • Access your data — Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct your data — Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Delete your data — Request deletion of your personal information from our systems
  • Withdraw consent — Withdraw SMS or marketing consent at any time without affecting any prior lawful processing
  • Opt out of cookies — Disable non-essential cookies through your browser settings
  • Data portability — Request your personal data in a portable format

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at (346) 660-2404 or jmartinta@freshcoatpainters.com. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

No Sharing — Critical

No Sharing of SMS Opt-In Data

Mobile phone numbers and SMS opt-in consent collected through any of our forms will never be shared with, sold to, rented to, or transferred to any third party or affiliate for marketing or promotional purposes under any circumstances.

SMS opt-in data is used exclusively for direct communications between Fresh Coat Painters of Cypress (D&JM Multiservices LLC) and the individual who opted in.

The only third parties permitted to process your phone number are SMS delivery platform providers, who are contractually prohibited from using your data for any purpose other than delivering our messages to you on our behalf.
