Program

About This SMS Program

Fresh Coat Painters of Cypress (operated by D&JM Multiservices LLC, 18223 Grove Brook Lane, Cypress, TX 77429) operates an SMS messaging program to communicate with customers and prospective customers regarding painting services.

This program covers the following messaging use cases:

  • Estimate & Appointment Confirmations — Confirming scheduled free estimates and consultations
  • Appointment Reminders — Reminder messages sent prior to a scheduled visit
  • Estimate Follow-Ups — Follow-up communications related to a submitted estimate request or proposal
  • Promotional Offers — Occasional promotional messages about services, seasonal specials, or discounts available to opted-in contacts
  • Customer Reactivation — Outreach to past customers regarding new services, project opportunities, or returning customer offers
  • Project Updates — Transactional updates related to an active or upcoming painting project

Messages are sent from numbers operated by Fresh Coat Painters of Cypress (D&JM Multiservices LLC).

Eligibility

Age Requirement

This SMS program is intended for individuals who are 18 years of age or older. By opting in to receive SMS messages from Fresh Coat Painters of Cypress, you confirm that you are at least 18 years old. We do not knowingly send marketing or promotional SMS messages to individuals under the age of 18.

Consent

How You Opt In

You consent to receive SMS messages from Fresh Coat Painters of Cypress when you submit a lead generation form — including forms on our website, Facebook page, or Instagram page — requesting a free painting estimate or other service inquiry. By submitting your phone number through any of these forms, you are explicitly agreeing to receive SMS communications from us.

No messages are sent before you submit a form. Consent is not a condition of purchasing any service.

The opt-in form includes the following disclosure:

"By submitting this form, you agree to receive SMS messages from Fresh Coat Painters of Cypress at the phone number provided. Messages may include appointment confirmations, estimate follow-ups, promotional offers, and customer updates. Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out. Reply HELP for help."

Messages

What We Send

Depending on your relationship with us, you may receive the following types of messages:

  • A booking confirmation with a link to schedule your consultation
  • Appointment reminders prior to your scheduled visit
  • Follow-up communications related to your estimate or project
  • Promotional offers and seasonal specials for opted-in contacts
  • Customer reactivation messages for past customers
  • Project updates and transactional notifications

Message frequency varies based on your inquiry and customer status — typically 1 to 5 messages per conversation or campaign. Messages are sent during business hours only (8 AM – 8 PM local time).

Opt-Out

How to Stop Messages

Reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe. You will receive one final confirmation and no further messages will be sent.

Other opt-out keywords: CANCEL, END, QUIT, UNSUBSCRIBE.

To re-subscribe after opting out, reply START.

  • Your mobile phone number and SMS opt-in consent will never be shared with, sold to, or transferred to any third party or affiliate for marketing or promotional purposes.
  • Phone numbers are used solely to communicate with you about your estimate request, project communications, and service-related messages you have opted in to receive.
  • We do not use your phone number for any purpose unrelated to the services you requested or opted in to receive.
  • SMS opt-in consent is collected at the point of form submission and stored securely. Opt-out requests are processed immediately.
