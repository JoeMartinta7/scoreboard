SMS Program

Description of SMS Program & Messaging Use Cases

Fresh Coat Painters of Cypress (D&JM Multiservices LLC) operates an SMS messaging program to communicate with customers and opted-in prospective customers. This SMS program is operated under the brand name Fresh Coat Painters of Cypress by the legal entity D&JM Multiservices LLC.

This SMS program covers the following messaging use cases:

  • Appointment Confirmations — Transactional SMS confirming free estimate appointments and consultations
  • Appointment Reminders — Reminder messages sent before a scheduled visit
  • Estimate Follow-Ups — Follow-up messages related to a submitted estimate or delivered proposal
  • Promotional & Marketing Messages — Seasonal offers, discounts, and service announcements (marketing opt-in required)
  • Customer Reactivation — Outreach to past customers regarding new services or returning customer offers (marketing opt-in required)
  • Project Updates — Transactional updates related to an active or scheduled painting project

Message frequency varies — typically 1 to 5 messages per inquiry or campaign. Messages are sent during business hours only (8 AM – 8 PM local time, Monday through Saturday).

Eligibility

Age Restriction — 18 Years or Older Required

This SMS program is intended for individuals who are 18 years of age or older (18+). By opting in to receive SMS messages from Fresh Coat Painters of Cypress, you confirm that you are at least 18 years old. We do not knowingly send SMS messages to individuals under the age of 18. If we become aware that a recipient is under 18, they will be immediately removed from all SMS communications.

Opt-Out Instructions

How to Stop Messages — Text STOP

STOP

Text STOP to any message from Fresh Coat Painters of Cypress to immediately unsubscribe and stop all SMS messages.

After texting STOP, you will receive one final confirmation message and no further messages will be sent to your number. Your opt-out request is processed immediately and permanently.

Additional opt-out keywords accepted: CANCEL, END, QUIT, UNSUBSCRIBE.

To re-subscribe after opting out, text START to any previous number you received a message from.

You may also opt out by contacting us directly at (346) 660-2404 or jmartinta@freshcoatpainters.com and requesting removal from SMS communications.
