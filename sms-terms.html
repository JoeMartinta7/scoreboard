Program

About This SMS Program

Fresh Coat Painters of Cypress (operated by D&JM Multiservices LLC, 18223 Grove Brook Lane, Cypress, TX 77429) operates an SMS messaging program to communicate with customers and prospective customers who have requested a free painting estimate.

Messages are sent from (346) 540-4868.

Consent

How You Opt In

You consent to receive SMS messages from Fresh Coat Painters of Cypress when you submit a lead generation form — including forms on our Facebook or Instagram pages — requesting a free painting estimate. By submitting your phone number through any of these forms, you are explicitly agreeing to receive SMS communications from us.

No messages are sent before you submit a form. Consent is not a condition of purchasing any service.

The opt-in form includes the following disclosure:

"By submitting this form, you agree to receive SMS messages from Fresh Coat Painters of Cypress at the phone number provided. Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out."

Messages

What We Send

After you request a free estimate, you may receive:

• A booking confirmation with a link to schedule your consultation
• Appointment reminders prior to your scheduled visit
• Follow-up communications related to your estimate or project

Message frequency varies based on your inquiry — typically 1 to 5 messages per conversation. We do not send unsolicited promotional blasts.

Opt-Out

How to Stop Messages

Reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe. You will receive one final confirmation and no further messages will be sent.

Other opt-out keywords: CANCEL, END, QUIT, UNSUBSCRIBE.

To re-subscribe after opting out, reply START.
